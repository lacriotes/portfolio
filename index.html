✦ Our Story

About Rice Palace

Hotel Rice Palace

Hotel Rice Palace is a family-run lodge and restaurant in Bharatpur-1, Chitwan — a place built on the simple idea that good food, a clean room and warm hospitality should always go together.

We are a family business. The people who welcome you at the door, the hands that prepare your meal, the voices that answer the phone — they are all part of the same home. That is what we mean when we say you are staying with us, not just at us.

Our Thakali Bhansa Ghar serves traditional Thakali cuisine — the food of the Thakali community, prepared with honest ingredients and the care of a family kitchen. It is the heart of our hotel, and the reason many of our guests return.

Our lodge sits directly beside the highway, making it an easy stop for travelers passing through Chitwan — for a night, for a meal, or for a longer stay. We welcome families, students, business guests, highway travelers and anyone looking for a comfortable place to rest.

We believe in simple things: clean rooms, hygienic food, fair prices, and treating every guest the way we would treat our own family. That is the promise of Hotel Rice Palace.
